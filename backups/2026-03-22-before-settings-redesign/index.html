📚 Study Note

App Tour

Getting Started

Choose the session type that fits how you want to practice, revise weak areas, or work under timed pressure.

Study Mode

Study Mode is for practicing questions at your own pace. You can focus on a single category such as cardiovascular, pulmonary, or any other available topic, or choose a mixed session when you want broader coverage. It also includes weak practice, which lets you return to questions you previously got wrong in study sessions.

Exam Mode and Smart Exam

Exam Mode follows the same question structure as Study Mode, but under a time limit. You can choose how many questions you want to answer, such as 30, 50, 100, or more, and you can run the exam by category or as a mixed paper. Smart Exam is available for practicing weak questions in a more targeted way when you want timed revision around your weaker areas.

Quick Drills

Quick Drills are short focused rounds. Rapid gives you 5 random questions to answer in 60 seconds. Sudden keeps going until the first wrong answer. Clinical gives you 10 solid case-scenario questions for more deliberate clinical thinking.

Daily Quiz

Daily Quiz gives you 10 random questions each day. Completing the daily set helps you build streaks and accumulate gems while keeping your revision consistent.

Question Flow

Each session follows a simple answer-review-progress workflow.

Answer and move through the session

Work through questions in order using the session controls. The interface keeps the question, answers, progress, and timing information close together so you can stay focused.

Open explanations when needed

After answering, use the explanation area to review the reasoning behind the correct option. This is the main place to correct misunderstandings before moving on.

Finish with review and results

Every session ends with a result view. Use it to see how you performed, identify weaker areas, and decide whether to continue practicing or switch to study tools.

Study Tools

Use the supporting tools between sessions to build stronger coverage.

Topic Library

Browse study notes by subject and open reading material directly inside the app. This is useful before a session when you want preparation, and after a session when you want reinforcement.

Performance Dashboard

Review overall results, patterns, and weaker areas over time. The dashboard is best used as a follow-up tool after several practice sessions.

AI Explanation

Use AI Explanation when you need a deeper breakdown of the answer logic. It is intended to supplement, not replace, the standard explanation panel.

Account and Settings

Use the top-right tools to manage account details and app preferences.

Profile

Keep your personal details and training identity current so your workspace remains organised around your account.

Settings

Adjust theme, text size, font, and motion preferences to make the app more comfortable during longer sessions.

Continue to the workspace

When you finish this guide, return to the menu and continue with study, exam, drills, topics, analytics, or account management as needed.
